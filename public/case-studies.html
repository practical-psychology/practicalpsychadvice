Case Studies

Editorial write-ups showing how three readers applied a specific book to a specific problem — the same problem-first, no-jargon approach every book in the series takes.

Andy Crawford

Educational Consultant

The problem

Andy ran workshops for new teachers but felt his own presence undercut his message — quiet rooms, unconvinced nods, no real buy-in.

The approach

He worked through the presence and social-cue chapters of The Secrets of Charisma over two weeks, applying one exercise per session he led.

The result

Post-workshop “instructor confidence” feedback scores rose within a month, and Andy reported feeling less like he was performing and more like he was simply present.

Read The Secrets of Charisma →

Ben Pantock

Licensed Social Worker

The problem

Ben was absorbing his clients’ emotional intensity during sessions and burning out faster than expected in his first two years of practice.

The approach

He used Mastering Emotional Intelligence to build a repeatable routine for naming and regulating his own reactions between sessions, not just during them.

The result

He describes steadier energy across a full caseload and fewer end-of-week crashes — the same empathy, without the same cost.

Read Mastering Emotional Intelligence →

Henry Letham

Entrepreneur

The problem

Henry’s startup was growing, but two key working relationships were fraying under pressure — miscommunication was quietly costing him trust.

The approach

He read Building and Maintaining Relationships specifically for the repair and boundary-setting chapters, and used the reflection exercises before his next two difficult conversations.

The result

Both relationships stabilized within weeks; Henry credits the shift to naming the pattern out loud instead of avoiding it.
