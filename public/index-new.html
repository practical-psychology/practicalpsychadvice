About the author

Andy Horowitz

Andy Horowitz created the Practical Psychology series to turn useful psychological ideas into short, readable guides for everyday life. Each book takes on one practical area — confidence, communication, emotions, relationships or motivation — and gives you tools you can actually use.

The approach is deliberately unglamorous: no grand transformation promises, no jargon for its own sake. Just the specific behaviours, patterns and exercises that make a real difference in ordinary situations.

Frequently asked questions

Who are these books for?

General readers who want psychology they can use — at work, in conversations, in relationships, and under pressure. They are written for people who want practical tools rather than academic theory.

Do I need any psychology background?

No. Every idea is explained in plain language, with everyday examples. If a technical term is needed, it is defined the first time it appears.

What format are the books, and what can I read them on?

Each title is an ebook, distributed through Draft2Digital to major retailers. You can read on Apple Books, Kobo, and other apps that support standard ebook formats, on a phone, tablet, e-reader or computer. Your file format and reading app depend on the store you buy from.

How long is each book?

Each guide covers one topic and is deliberately kept short — the goal is a book you finish and use, not one you abandon halfway.

How much does each book cost?

Every title in the series is $6.99. There is no subscription and no upsell required — each book stands on its own.

Which book should I start with?

Start with the problem you want to solve rather than book one. Use the “Start with the problem you want to solve” section above — it points each common situation to the right guide.

Can I give a book as a gift?

Yes. Most ebook retailers, including Apple Books and Kobo, support gifting. Use the retailer links on any book and follow that store’s gifting process.

What if the book isn’t right for me?

Email info@practicalpsychadvice.com within 30 days and we will make it right. If you bought through Apple Books, Kobo or another retailer, refunds are processed under that store’s policy — write to us and we will help you through it.

Is this therapy, or a substitute for mental health care?

No. These are educational and practical guides. They are not therapy, diagnosis or treatment, and they are not a substitute for care from a qualified mental health professional. If you are struggling, please talk to a doctor or therapist.
